Program memory

Program display: Speed:

Listings may be written in mnemonics (STO 1, ST+ 1, X≥Y? or X>=Y?, GTO 07) or in HP-25 keycodes (23 51 01), with or without step numbers; up to 49 steps. Memory, registers and settings are kept in this browser; the power switch OFF clears them, as on the real HP-25.

  • In PRGM mode the display shows 04 STO 1 instead of 04 23 01. Keying an instruction writes it into the next step (the HP-25 overwrites; it does not insert). SST/BST move through memory.
  • In RUN mode: f PRGM resets to step 00, GTO nn positions the program, R/S runs it, SST executes one step. Unused steps hold GTO 00; reaching step 00 stops the program.
  • Σ+ accumulates in R3 (n), R4 (Σy), R5 (Σxy), R6 (Σx²), R7 (Σx); f x̄ and f s use them. A storage overflow shows OF.
